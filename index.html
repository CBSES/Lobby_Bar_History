★ League Manager's Note · 16 Year Retrospective ★

Sixteen Years. Same Ten Idiots. Zero Roster Changes.

— The totally not AI-written, 16-years-in-the-making League Manager's Note —

Let me set the scene. It's 2010. Obama is president, the iPad just came out, and ten guys decided to start a fantasy football league. That same ten guys are still here in 2025. No turnover. No replacement owners. No "my friend's brother needs a team." Sixteen seasons of the same trash talk, the same draft-night chaos, the same suspicious "my phone died" excuses on Sunday morning lineup deadlines.

And what do we have to show for it? Nine different champions. Sixteen rings spread across nine guys. Only one owner has never tasted glory — and you already know who that is. Neil finally got his first ring this year after sixteen years of begging. Andrew won three of the first four titles and then promptly disappeared for twelve years like the post-2010 Saints. Eric Jalbert has the best regular-season win percentage in league history and exactly one championship to show for it. He's been runner-up FOUR times. He's the Falcons of the Lobby Bar — leads the league all season then can't seal the deal in February.

Greg wins championships when nobody expects it (2015, 2024 — the year he finished 8-5 with a -19 PF/G differential against him), because Greg basically re-drafts his entire team off the waiver wire by week 6 (he gets 41% of his points from FA pickups, the highest in the league). Andrew leaves nineteen points on his bench every single week and somehow has three rings. Robert has named his team "I hate fantasy Football" for EIGHT CONSECUTIVE SEASONS (2018-2025). He won the chip in 2021 with that team name. Apparently the strategy works. The math doesn't math.

Special shoutout to Matt Howard, who has finished dead last in three consecutive seasons (2023-2024-2025). That's not a slump, Matt — that's a lifestyle choice. The same Matt who once named his team "Big Dick Bandits" three years in a row (2015, 2016, 2017) and "Kickers are players too" twice (2022, 2023). Matt commits to the bit. Just not the football.

Special shoutout to TJ Doody, who has milked his own surname for sixteen years of "Doody" puns — CALLOF DOODY (2011), I TOUCHDOWN THERE (2012), Doody Blues (five straight years from 2018-2022), and now Rudy doody for the last three running. He finally cracked the playoffs in 2025 after a twelve-year drought and finished 3rd. The cellar dweller has risen. Until next year, when he probably finishes 9th again. He'll be back. Probably with another Doody pun.

Sixteen years. 199 regular season games per owner. ~22,000 points scored. No one has quit. That's either a testament to friendship or a hostage situation. We're not sure anymore.

— League Manager · Roasting commences below ↓

Things The Numbers Reveal

DEEPER PATTERNS · 16 YEAR ANALYSIS

Andrew's 2,000-Point Season

2019
In 2019, Andrew Howard scored 2,000.5 PF — the only owner to ever crack 2,000 in a single season. Went 10-3. Finished 3rd. That's right — most points in league history, but lost in the playoffs. Eric Jalbert (1,771) finished 2nd. Chad Brodeur (1,669) won it all. Lobby Bar fantasy football: where points don't matter as much as showing up in week 16.

The 2017 Score Recession

DARK YEAR
2017 had the lowest scoring in league history. TJ Doody went 0-12 with 929 PF — the only winless season in 16 years. Matt Howard went 2-10 with 924 PF — the lowest single-season PF on record. Eric Jalbert finished last with 1,105 PF and a .250 win pct. Two future champs at the bottom. Sometimes the football gods are cruel to everyone.

The Pluciennik Sibling Rivalry

FAMILY DRAMA
Thomas and Robert Pluciennik have played 13 times in regular season + playoffs. Robert leads the all-time series, but they've split the championships 2-1 in Thomas's favor (2014, 2023 vs Robert's 2021). Thomas dominates Robert head-to-head in the regular season, but Robert won the only ring head-to-head matchup. Thanksgiving must be... interesting.

Matt Howard's Triple Toilet

2023-24-25
Three consecutive last-place finishes. 2023, 2024, 2025 — all 10th. That's an entire managerial collapse documented for posterity. Matt's two championships came in 2012 and 2022. Statistical bookends to a career that, charitably, has had a lot of "rebuilding years." Less charitably, has had a lot of not even trying.

Greg The Waiver Wire Wizard

41% FROM FA
Greg Jalbert gets 41.4% of his points from waiver/free agent pickups — the highest in the league. He doesn't draft well; he just finds guys. Andrew is right behind at 40.1%. Compare that to Thomas Pluciennik, who sits on his draft (only 19% from waivers). Greg has 2 chips. Thomas has 2 chips. Different paths, same destination.

Andrew Leaves Points On The Bench

86.9% EFF
Andrew Howard has the worst coaching efficiency in the league at 86.9%. He leaves ~19 points per week on the bench. That's 1,856 wasted points across 7 seasons. And he STILL has 3 championships. Imagine if he actually set his lineups correctly. He'd have 8 rings.

The Sean Henry Hangover

2020 → 2021
Sean Henry won the championship in 2020 going 7-5. The very next season? 2-11. Last place. Worst championship hangover in league history. He has 4 playoff appearances total — went 4-0 in playoff games (148.9 PPG, the highest playoff scoring rate of any owner). When he shows up in the playoffs, he wins. When he doesn't show up... he doesn't show up.

The Chad Coaster

±10 SWING
Most volatile owner in the league. 3 championships (2016, 2017, 2019) — including the only back-to-back run with finishes of 1st and 1st. 3 last-place finishes (2013, 2020, 2022). Highest standard deviation of any owner in final standings. When Chad is on, he's unbeatable. When he's off, he's the worst team on the field. Unlike most owners — there's no middle ground.

★ Final Word ★

Here's To Year 17.

Sixteen years down. Ten guys still arguing about whether Tony Romo is/was a top-12 fantasy QB. Sixteen draft nights. Sixteen championship Sundays. Probably hundreds of "I had Burrow on my bench" texts. We've watched players come and go. We've watched coaches come and go. We've stayed exactly the same.

Andrew is still leaving points on his bench. Eric is still finishing second. TJ is still in the cellar (most years). Greg is still scouring the waiver wire at 11:58 PM Saturday night. Thomas is still drafting his entire roster in round one. Neil finally won. Matt is rebuilding (always). Robert is "gonna be back next year." Sean had that one championship. Chad is somewhere on the spectrum from God-tier to last place — and you don't know which until December.

Whatever happens in 2026, the same ten of you will be here. Don't change a damn thing.

— The League · 2010-2025 · Forever Lobby Bar
